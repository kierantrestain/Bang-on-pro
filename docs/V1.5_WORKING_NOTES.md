## Research reuse (patterns only, no code copied, no framework installed)
Availability and licences were checked on GitHub: ACE (Apache-2.0), AWM (Apache-2.0), GEPA (MIT), Reflexion (MIT).
- **ACE** (primary): an evolving playbook of itemised lessons with incremental delta updates, never a wholesale rewrite. It separates roles: Generator (specialists), Reflector (QC), Curator (Learning); our Executive retrieves. Items are deduplicated by key with helpful/harmful counters, which map to our `support`, `contradict` and value histogram. Retrieval is bounded.
- **AWM**: induces reusable workflows from successful trajectories by abstracting example-specific values. Here, concrete times, cameras, speakers and files become anchors such as the turn role/rank/point, the camera role, the speaker role and an asset input.
- **GEPA**: reflective mutation from execution feedback; candidates are evaluated on separate feedback, validation and held-out sets, under a small candidate budget. Here, deterministic reflection maps a validated lesson to a whitelisted setting; at most one optional LLM proposal comes out of the shared budget.
- **Reflexion**: persistent verbal lessons from failures, given back on the next attempt. Here, a failure lesson comes only from a QC-verified correction, never from an agent's own claim.

## Design decisions
- One SQLite file (WAL), stdlib only, with indexes on scope, agent and status, plus keyword-overlap ranking. No embeddings or vector DB, and no new dependency.
- Experience is captured at task completion, as one transaction with idempotent ids. Live hooks only add the compound-tool `step` and `rejected` events to the task.
- Strategies are evaluated **in memory**: the edit layer is replayed with the scenario steps, then the independent `check_draft` runs. Nothing is written, and the draft revision does not change.
- The skill-first path runs before reasoning: a match runs with no LLM call and is undone if it fails to verify. A candidate is never retried on the production it came from.
- Failover is app-driven only. MCP clients keep control of their own sessions.
