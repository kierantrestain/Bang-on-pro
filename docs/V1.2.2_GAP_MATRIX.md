# Bang On Pro V1.2.2: gap matrix (start of the internal-beta refinement)

Baseline: `v1.2.1` (`dbaf0dc`). Python QA: 95 passed, 0 failed, 1 skipped (local Linux container, re-run at the start of V1.2.2).
Every finding below comes from reading the V1.2.1 code. Owner: **E** editorial intelligence, **M** media processing, **R** production readiness, **L** lead (integration).

## Weak agent decisions

| Agent | V1.2.1 behaviour found in code | Gap | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive | Regex brief flags plus mode widening. Confidence is the interpreter's single number. No final verification beyond source hashes | No conflict resolution (e.g. "keep every word" with "tight cut"), no per-agent dependency confidence, no final draft verification against the brief | E (+L hooks) |
| Media Ingest | Role taken from the filename regex; the probe only reads codec, size and fps | No camera/microphone identification (make, model, timecode, channel layout), no duplicate detection, no validation of corrupt or truncated files, VFR or audio-only streams | M |
| Synchronisation | One GCC-PHAT offset per source from one bounded window | No clock-drift detection or correction for long recordings. Interrupted recordings are only detected. No per-segment re-sync | M |
| Editorial Intelligence | Transcript, energy-based sections, and a highlight z-score of energy, turn density, overlap and a keyword list | No topic segmentation, laughter/humour, reaction or interruption classification, or significant-moment typing. Highlight scoring has no context | E |
| Multicam Director | Speakers mapped by microphone dominance; widest angle used as the wide | No conversational dynamics (who is listening, reaction timing), no camera availability (black/frozen/missing segments), no cut-frequency control | E |
| Rough Cut | **Every** interior silence longer than the threshold is excised (`_compute_kept_master_intervals`) | Removes meaningful pauses (after questions, before punchlines, reactions, laughter). No repetition or false-start removal. Pauses are cut out instead of compressed to a natural length | E |
| Audio Engineer | Fixed chain: HPF, expander or RNNoise, compand, per-mic trim, loudnorm | No clipping detection, no crosstalk ducking or bleed handling, no measured microphone matching (spectral/level), no intelligibility check | M |
| Colourist | Global per-camera RGB-mean gain to the reference | No white balance from neutrals, no skin-tone consistency, no shot-level correction, no clamp that preserves creative intent | M |
| Graphics | Lower thirds from the speaker map; title card | Not context-aware (first appearance, topic changes), no safe-area validation, no minimum and maximum on-screen timing, no reusable template registry | E |
| Captions | Cues from the transcript, cut to kept intervals; withheld without neural ASR | No custom terminology or proper-noun correction, no punctuation repair, no speaker-change markers, no continuity re-check after timeline edits | E |
| VFX | One static matte from one representative frame per camera | No temporal propagation, no mask-consistency (IoU) check, no occlusion or edge handling. Runs on every shot even when the subject does not move (wasted compute) | M |
| Quality Control | Technical QC plus shot length, jump cuts, caption speed, sync confidence and coverage | No detection of repeated content, missing context, awkward cuts (mid-word), sync drift, camera-availability or caption-accuracy problems | E + M (hooks) |
| Distribution | Centre-crop 9:16, energy highlights, loudness two-pass on the master | Vertical crop ignores the subject. No per-platform clip length or pacing. Export profiles not validated. Delivery verification limited to loudness | M (renders) / E (selection) |
| Learning | Accept/reject records, bounded suggestions, versioned overlays | No draft→final comparison beyond the CLI diff, no detection of recurring preferences across jobs, no safe import/export between editors | R |
